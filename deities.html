Dharma / forms and stories

Many forms of the divine.

Deities are approached through different traditions, relationships and stories. These introductions are starting points, not fixed definitions.

BEGINNINGS & WISDOM

Ganesha

Associated with auspicious beginnings, learning and the removal of obstacles.

PRESERVATION & DEVOTION

Vishnu

A preserver of cosmic order, known through forms such as Rama and Krishna.

TRANSFORMATION

Shiva

A complex figure connected with meditation, destruction, renewal and grace.

THE DIVINE FEMININE

Devi

The goddess in her many forms, including Durga, Lakshmi, Saraswati and Kali.

LOVE & TEACHING

Krishna

A beloved form of Vishnu associated with devotion, music and the Bhagavad Gita.

DEVOTION & SERVICE

Hanuman

A model of courage, humility and unwavering devotion in the Ramayana.

Learning hubSacred texts

Hindu traditions differ in how deities are understood: as manifestations of one reality, personal forms of worship, family protectors or beloved figures. Context matters.

Stories are ways of thinking

Mythology is not only a list of supernatural events. Stories can express ethical dilemmas, devotion, relationships, the nature of time and the human search for meaning.
