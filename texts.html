Beyond the Vedas

UpanishadsBhagavad GitaRamayana & Mahabharata

Hindu sacred literature includes revealed texts, remembered traditions, epics, philosophical dialogue, poetry and living oral transmission.

Upanishads

The Upanishads explore the relationship between the self, ultimate reality and knowledge. Their questioning style makes them an important foundation for Vedanta.

Bhagavad Gita

Set within the Mahabharata, the Gita presents a dialogue on duty, action, devotion, knowledge and the possibility of freedom.

The great epics

The Ramayana and Mahabharata carry philosophical and ethical questions through memorable characters, conflict and stories of responsibility.
