Dharma / the sacred calendar

A year of living traditions.

Celebrations connect devotion, seasons, stories and community. Meanings and observances vary across regions.

01

Diwali

Light, renewal and diverse regional stories of hope and victory.

02

Holi

Colour, community and the arrival of spring.

03

Navaratri

Nine nights honouring the goddess in different forms.

04

Pongal

Thanksgiving for the harvest in Tamil traditions.

05

Ganesh Chaturthi

Celebrating Ganesha and new beginnings.

06

Thaipusam

A major Murugan festival marked by devotion, vows and procession.
