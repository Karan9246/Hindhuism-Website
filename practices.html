Dharma / everyday practice

Faith, in the everyday.

From a lamp lit at home to a community gathering, practice gives ideas a place in daily life.

01 / DEVOTION

Puja

Offerings and prayer at home or in a temple, often involving flowers, food, water, light and incense.

02 / LIGHT

Aarti

A lamp offered in reverence, often accompanied by song and prayer.

03 / SOUND

Mantra

Sacred utterances repeated or chanted with attention and intention.

04 / COMMUNITY

Seva

Service offered for the benefit of others without centring personal reward.

Rites of passage

Daily ritualsLife-cycle rituals

Daily rituals

Practices vary widely by family and tradition. Some people pray at dawn or dusk, meditate, recite mantras, visit a temple or mark a home shrine.

Life-cycle rituals

Samskaras may mark birth, naming, first feeding, education, marriage and death. Their forms differ across communities and regions.
