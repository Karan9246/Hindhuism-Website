Core concepts

DharmaKarmaSamsaraMokshaPaths of yoga

There is no single Hindu philosophical position. The ideas below recur across several traditions but are interpreted differently.

Dharma

Ethical responsibility, right conduct and the order that sustains life.

Karma

Action and consequence: choices shape character, experience and future.

Samsara

The continuing cycle of birth, death and rebirth.

Moksha

Liberation from samsara and realization of ultimate truth.

Four paths of yoga

Bhakti emphasises devotion; jnana, knowledge; karma yoga, selfless action; and raja yoga, meditation and discipline.
